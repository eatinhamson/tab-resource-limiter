CPU slowdown ×3 means the tab runs at one third speed. Network cap limits bandwidth (choose "none" to leave it alone). Discard frees the tab's RAM after N minutes of inactivity (0 = never); the page reloads when you return to it. "Background only" lifts the throttle while the tab is focused.
